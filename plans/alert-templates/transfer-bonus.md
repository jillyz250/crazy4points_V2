# Transfer Bonus — alert template

For "Program X is transferring to Program Y with an N% bonus" alerts. Owner: John;
facts verified by Priya. **Legal tier: skip Charlie** (everyday points news) UNLESS
the alert also promotes a card or carries an affiliate link, in which case it escalates
to the card rules + Charlie.

## When to use it
A points/miles transfer bonus between two programs, for a limited window. NOT a card
sign-up bonus (that's `card-bonus-increase.md`), not a buy-points sale (that's the
award-sale template).

## The sections
1. **Lead** — one line: `X → Y`, the bonus %, the deadline, and the hook (what it unlocks).
2. **The deal** — the ratio, stated as **normal → boosted** (e.g., "1:1 normally, 1:1.3 with the bonus"), the transfer **direction** (it's one-way), the **end date**, and any **minimum transfer**. If the pair is NOT 1:1 even with the bonus, say so plainly (the Cap One → JAL lesson: 1,000 becomes 975, not 1,000).
3. **The sweet spot** — ⭐ a **specific, verified redemption the bonus makes reachable** (a real award at a real level, from `sweet_spots`). Shown, not told. Qualitative only, no cents-per-point. This is the whole reason to care.
4. **Good deal, or wait?** — how this % compares to the **typical range for THIS pair** (verified from history, e.g. "Amex → Hilton runs 20 to 40 percent a few times a year, so 30 is average"). Give an honest jump-or-wait read. Never predict a specific future bonus as guaranteed.
5. **The catch** — ⭐ **transfers are ONE-WAY and FINAL.** Only transfer with a **specific redemption in mind** (confirm the award space is bookable FIRST). Repeat the non-1:1 ratio if applicable, and the minimum.
6. **Bottom line** — who this is for, and the hard rule restated: **don't transfer speculatively; move points only for an award you're ready to book.**

## Hard standards (non-negotiable)
- **Never transfer without a redemption** (`feedback_never_transfer_without_redemption`) — §5 and §6 must carry this; it's the #1 way readers get burned.
- **Real sweet spot (§3)** — specific + verified, never "points go far."
- **Anchor §4 on the typical range for the pair** — so "good deal" is honest, not hype. If we don't know the typical range, verify it or say it plainly ("bonuses on this pair are rare/uncommon"), don't guess.
- **Verify award availability** for the sweet spot before implying it's bookable.
- Global rules: no em/en dashes; no foreign-currency valuations; no derived point math; figures Priya-verified.

## Disclosure
None required (no affiliate link, no card). If a card is mentioned or an affiliate/referral
link is added, it becomes card-tier: append the editorial disclosure and route to Charlie
(see `card-bonus-increase.md` §7 + two-eyes-policy Rule 4).
