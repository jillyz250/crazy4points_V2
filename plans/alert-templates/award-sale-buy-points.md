# Award Sale / Buy Points — alert template

For discounts on redemptions (award_sale) and bonuses/discounts on buying points
(point_purchase). Owner: John; facts verified by Priya. **Legal tier: skip Charlie.**

## The sections
1. **Lead** — the discount or bonus + the deadline + the hook.
2. **The deal** — the exact terms: award sale % off, or buy-points bonus/discount and the
   effective price, plus the deadline and any purchase cap.
3. **Is the price good?** — a qualitative read against the program's usual floor ("near the
   cheapest this program sells points," or "a routine discount"). NO cents-per-point, NO
   dollar valuation, NO derived math (house rule) — keep it comparative and honest.
4. **⭐ Only buy for a redemption you'll book** — the hard rule: never buy points speculatively.
   Name the **specific sweet spot** the purchase unlocks and confirm the award is bookable first.
5. **The catch** — purchases are non-refundable and final; a "sale" price can still be a bad
   deal if you have no use for the points.
6. **Bottom line** — who should buy, for what, by when.

## Hard standards (non-negotiable)
- **Never buy/redeem speculatively** — buy only for an award you're ready to book
  (`feedback_never_transfer_without_redemption` applies to buying too). §4 carries this.
- **Real, verified sweet spot** the purchase enables — never generic.
- **No foreign-currency valuations, no derived point-to-dollar math**; price framed
  qualitatively vs the program's own floor. No dashes; figures Priya-verified.
- **`end_date` = the deadline** (auto-expires; closed-loop).
