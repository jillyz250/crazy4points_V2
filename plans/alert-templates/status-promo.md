# Status Promo — alert template

For elite-status offers: status matches, fast-tracks, status challenges, extensions.
Covers status_promo. Owner: John; facts verified by Priya. **Legal tier: skip Charlie.**

## The sections
1. **Lead** — the status offer + the deadline + the one-line benefit.
2. **The offer** — what it is (a **match**, a **fast-track**, or a **challenge**), the tier
   you land, the requirements, and the timeframe.
3. **Who benefits** — who should grab it (a frequent traveler in that program or region,
   someone holding matchable status elsewhere).
4. **What the status actually gets you** — the concrete perks that make it worth it
   (lounge access, upgrades, free bags, priority) — the "sweet spot" of the status.
5. **The catch** — is it a soft **match** (easy) or a **challenge** (you must FLY/spend to
   keep it)? Does the matched status expire? One-time only? Registration required?
6. **Bottom line** — who should act, by when.

## Hard standards (non-negotiable)
- **Distinguish a MATCH from a CHALLENGE** — a match is near-free; a challenge requires real
  flying/spend. Never blur them; that's the reader's biggest decision.
- **Concrete perks in §4** — verified benefits of the tier, not "great status."
- Verify the match/challenge terms against the program's official page.
- **`end_date` = the registration deadline** (auto-expires; closed-loop).
- No valuations, no dashes; figures Priya-verified.
