## What this is
A plain-English map of every hotel and travel-portal "best rate / price match" guarantee — who has one, what you actually get, and how to win a claim. We researched **27 hotel loyalty programs** and **14 travel portals** and pulled the terms from each company's **own official pages** (not blogs). Where a program couldn't be confirmed from an official source, we left it off rather than guess.

## ⚡ Quick takeaways
- **Best overall:** Marriott & Hilton — match **+ 25% off**.
- **Best cash reward:** Best Western — match **+ $100 gift card**.
- **Best points option:** Marriott & Hyatt — match **+ 5,000 points** (or take the % off).
- **Tightest minimum gap:** Accor — the competing rate must be at least **5% (or €5) lower** (most chains just need $1).
- **Most flexible travel portal:** Capital One Travel — 100% of the difference on flights, hotels *and* cars.

> **One thing to know up front:** a "best rate guarantee" is not a guarantee you'll get a lower price. Approval depends on meeting **every** requirement in that chain's official terms — matching room type, dates, occupancy, cancellation policy, taxes/fees, and live availability. Miss one and it's denied.

## How to actually win a best rate guarantee
Every program runs on the same five rules — get these right and most claims go through:
1. **Book direct first** (as a loyalty member where required) — you almost always need a confirmed booking before you can claim.
2. **Claim within 24 hours** of booking (some chains also require it a set number of days *before* check-in).
3. **Match everything** — same hotel, room type, dates, occupancy, and especially the **same cancellation policy**. Refundable-vs-prepaid mismatch is the #1 reason claims get denied.
4. **Use a public, bookable rate** — no member-only, login, coupon, opaque (Priceline/Hotwire), package, or group rates.
5. **Compare room rate only**, net of taxes and resort fees, and mind the **minimum gap** (usually $1 or 1%; Accor needs 5%). *(Note: Hyatt's often-cited "3%" only applies when the competing rate is in a different currency — a normal same-currency claim just needs $1.)*

## A quick worked example
Say you hold a refundable **Hyatt** booking at **$153/night** and spot the same refundable room, same dates, on **Expedia for $143** (both in USD). The competing rate just has to be at least **$1 lower** — $10 clears that easily — and because both are refundable, the cancellation policies match. That's a clean, qualifying claim: Hyatt matches the $143 and you pick your reward (5,000 points or 20% off). Miss any one of those conditions — a non-refundable competing rate, a member-only price, a resort-fee difference — and it's denied.
