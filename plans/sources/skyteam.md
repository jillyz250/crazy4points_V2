## Notable 2024-2026 events

- **SAS joined SkyTeam September 1, 2024** (departed Star Alliance August 31, 2024). Alliance Adherence Agreement signed April 29, 2024.
- **Aeroflot suspension** — "SkyTeam and Aeroflot have agreed to temporarily suspend the airline's SkyTeam membership" (effective 2022, ongoing as of April 2026).
- **April 1, 2025** — SkyTeam Elite Plus domestic-only lounge access expanded at select participating member lounges.
- **January 1, 2024** — Delta Sky Club access tightened: SkyMiles members in Basic Economy lose access; Diamond/Platinum/Gold Medallion in Main Cabin or Comfort+ on international itineraries lose complimentary Delta Sky Club access.
- **2024** — Aeromexico transitioning Club Premier to Aeromexico Rewards branding (rollout uneven across surfaces).
- **March 13, 2023** — Virgin Atlantic joined SkyTeam.
