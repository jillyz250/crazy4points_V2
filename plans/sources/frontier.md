| 2026-05-05 | "Major bases at LAS/MCO/MIA/ATL/PHX" | Both Copilot and ChatGPT flagged ❓ UNVERIFIED | Pulled back from intro/quirks. Hubs only lists DEN. Frontier operates point-to-point without traditional hubs at other cities. |
| 2026-05-05 | "Frontier card $99 AF, 5x flyfrontier / 3x dining / 1x other / 50K SUB" details on program page | Sonnet audit flagged HIGH - card-specific details belong on card page, not program page (per feedback_no_card_af_on_program_pages) | Reduced to generic "see card page" reference in quirks. |
