## Key verified facts (from official T&C, pasted by Jill)
- Earning: Gold 1 pt/USD, Jade 1.25, Diamond & Polaris 1.5.
- Tiers: Gold (free) / Jade (20 nights or 6,000 tier pts) / Diamond (50 or 15,000) / Polaris (invite).
- Redemption: FIXED 15 points = USD 1 (~6.67c/pt) for rooms, dining, spa, experiences, Boutique. NOT dynamic.
- Points expire: 2nd anniversary of accrual-year end (~25-36 mo). Account inactive after 36 mo.
- Airline conversion: min 1,000 pts, 500 increments, 4-6 wks. Base 1:1; KrisFlyer 1:1.25; China Southern + Hainan 1:1.6.
- OTA bookings (Expedia/Booking.com/Trip.com) do NOT earn points or benefits.
- Inbound: Atmos (via Atmos Summit card hotel transfer) - ratio still unverified.
