## SAS departure (legacy reference, August 2024)

- SAS exited Star Alliance August 31, 2024 after 27 years; joined SkyTeam September 1, 2024. Star Alliance press release dated April 8, 2024 announcing SAS exit. Captured on Star Alliance page as a 25-member alliance from September 1, 2024 through ITA's join in April 2026.

## Korean Air–Asiana merger context

- Merger closed December 2024. Korean Air is a SkyTeam member; Asiana remains a Star Alliance full member as of April 2026 with no announced exit timeline. Watch for transition announcements over 2-3 years.
