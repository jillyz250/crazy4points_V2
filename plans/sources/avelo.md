- **Two-program structure is unusual** - Avelo Rewards (card-only earn) and Avelo PLUS (paid subscription) operate as parallel products. If Avelo ever adds a flying-based earn track to Rewards, the page will need a structural rewrite.
- **Cardless infrastructure** - Avelo is the first US airline co-brand on Cardless's digital-first card platform. Watch for other carriers following suit; if so, a "Cards on Cardless" cross-reference might become useful.
