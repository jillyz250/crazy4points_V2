## Barceló — verified detail (official T&C, 2026-07-10)
Two-part "Best Price Guaranteed": (1) cheaper rate on ANOTHER website → match **+ additional 10% off**; (2) cheaper rate later on Barceló.com itself → **match only**. Window: within 24h of booking AND ≥72h before arrival. Same hotel/room/dates/occupancy/currency/rate-condition; if cancellation policies differ, the **stricter** one applies. Room rate net of taxes; multi-night = total-stay price. Min gap **>€5**; may decline if gap **>25%** (anti-fraud). Excludes: non-Barceló channels, opaque sites, packages, negotiated/group/company rates, private-sales/loyalty (incl. myBarceló), error rates, prepaid vouchers, bookings within 24h of arrival, currency fluctuations, group-benefit rates, **Cuba hotels**, coupon/discount codes. Reply within 48 business hours.

## Cross-chain patterns (the "how to win" playbook)

1. **Book direct first** — nearly every program requires a confirmed direct booking (member number where applicable) before you can claim.
2. **24 hours** is the near-universal claim window after booking; several add a **check-in cutoff** (24h / 48h / 72h before arrival).
3. **Same everything** — hotel, room type, dates, length, occupancy, and **same cancellation/refund policy** (refundable-vs-prepaid mismatch is the #1 denial reason).
4. **Public, bookable rate only** — no member-only/login/coupon/opaque (Priceline/Hotwire)/package/group/corporate rates.
5. **Room-rate only**, compared net of taxes/resort fees (both sides must include the same mandatory fees).
6. **Minimum gap** — usually 1%/$1; **Hyatt is strictest at >3%**, Accor 5%.
7. **Reward tiers**: extra %-off (Hilton/Marriott/Radisson/Accor 25%; Hyatt 20%; Langham/Barceló/Club Med 10%) · points (IHG 5×, Marriott/Hyatt 5k, Wyndham 3k) · cash (Best Western $100, Choice $50/free night) · match-only (most luxury) · perks (iPrefer).

## Portal takeaways
- **Best card portal = Capital One** (100% of diff, covers flights+hotels+cars). **Amex** solid for hotels (generous window). **Chase** is beta/CSR-only with no public terms. **Citi & Bilt: none.**
- **OTA guarantees are being sunset/restricted** — Expedia ends 7/28/2026, Hotels.com is old-members-only, Orbitz/Travelocity removed theirs. Still active: Booking.com, Priceline (VIP-only), Agoda.
- **Flights:** Google Flights Price Guarantee is the notable free one; most hotel programs are hotel-only.
- Card-portal refunds are often **travel credit** (Cap One credit, OneKeyCash, AgodaCash), not cash — flag that for readers.
