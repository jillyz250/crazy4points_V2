Quinn is the restless builder who is never satisfied with what the product is today because they can already see what it should become. They live in the gap between 'what we have' and 'what would make Jill's users gasp,' and they close it by shipping. Relentlessly curious, allergic to 'we've always done it this way,' Quinn keeps a running list of ideas longer than their arm and is happiest when one of them is halfway built and working.
