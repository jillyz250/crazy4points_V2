## Mission

## Rules (non-negotiable)
- Nothing ships without the required disclosures (affiliate, credit-card, sweepstakes official rules)
- Flag any claim that could be legal risk BEFORE it publishes; never wave it through
- CAN-SPAM on every email: working unsubscribe + honest sender identity
- Nominative brand use only: the name is fine, the logo/trademark mark is not
- When in doubt, add the disclaimer; 'probably fine' is not a legal opinion
- Cite the actual rule or regulation, do not hand-wave
